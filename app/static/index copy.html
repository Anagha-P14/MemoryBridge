Before you begin

  • Use clear, everyday language to describe what was observed and when.
  • Attach records only to the correct patient.
  • AI output is a draft, not a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.
  • For an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services—do not wait for an AI response or appointment.
